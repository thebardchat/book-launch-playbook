## Tips
- Instagram: put hashtags in the first comment, not the caption
- Twitter: fewer is better — 2-3 max
- LinkedIn: hashtags go at the bottom
- Facebook: hashtags barely matter — write for the feed algorithm instead
- Reddit: no hashtags. Ever.
