# Case Study: Mistakes

> What we got wrong. Updated as we learn.

## Mistake 1: No email list before launch
**What happened:** Published the book with zero email subscribers. Every sale had to come from social media posts.
**The fix:** Build a landing page with an email signup BEFORE the book is ready. Collect interest. Launch to a list, not to the void.

## Mistake 2: Full manuscript in public repo
**What happened:** The entire book was in a public GitHub repo. Anyone could read it for free.
**The fix:** Made the manuscript repo private. Created a separate public process repo that shows HOW the book was made without giving away WHAT's in it.

## Mistake 3: No Goodreads profile
**What happened:** Didn't set up a Goodreads author profile before launch. Missed the whole Goodreads discovery ecosystem.
**The fix:** Set up Goodreads author page. Claim your book. Add it to relevant shelves.

## Mistake 4: No video content
**What happened:** All launch content was text-based. No book trailer, no TikTok, no Reels.
**The fix:** Record a 30-60 second video. It doesn't have to be professional. A phone, a face, a story. Video outperforms text on every platform.

## Mistake 5: Posting in communities cold
**What happened:** Dropped book links in communities we hadn't participated in yet.
**The fix:** Engage for at least a week before promoting. Be a person. Then be an author.
