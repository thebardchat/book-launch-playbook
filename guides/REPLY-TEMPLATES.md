# Reply Templates

Quick responses for comments and DMs. Copy-paste from your phone.

> [NUMBER] [GENRE] vignettes about [CORE THEME]. [ONE VIVID IMAGE]. [ANOTHER]. No names. You know these people.

## When someone says congratulations
> Thank you! It's been a wild ride — [ONE SENTENCE ABOUT THE PROCESS]. If you grab a copy I'd love to hear what you think.

## When someone asks how long it took
> The writing took [TIMEFRAME]. The figuring-out-how-to-write took [LONGER TIMEFRAME]. The second one was the real work.

## When someone asks about the AI
> I recorded raw voice — [YOUR METHOD]. [YOUR TOOL] shaped those into prose. I brought the life, [TOOL] brought the craft. Neither of us is the author alone.

## When someone asks the price
> It's on Amazon — paperback and ebook. Here's the link: [AMAZON LINK]

## When someone says "I could never write a book"
> I run concrete dispatch for a living. I recorded voice memos on my drive home and an AI helped me shape them. If I can do it, the barrier isn't talent. It's starting.

## When someone asks for writing advice
> Talk first. Write second. Record yourself saying the thing before you try to type the thing. Your speaking voice knows things your typing voice doesn't.

## When a troll says AI books don't count
> [Don't respond. Scroll past. The algorithm punishes arguments and rewards engagement from people who actually care. Spend your energy on the person who said congratulations instead.]

## When someone shares your post
> Reply with a genuine thank you. Tag them. These people are your street team. Treat them like gold.

## When someone leaves a review
> Screenshot it. Post it. Thank them publicly. One real review is worth more than a thousand impressions.
