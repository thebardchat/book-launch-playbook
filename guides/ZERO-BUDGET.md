# Zero-Budget Book Marketing

You have a phone. You have an internet connection. You have the truth. That's the budget.

## The Rules

1. **Your story IS the marketing.** You don't need a hook. You need to tell the truth about how the book got made. If the truth isn't interesting, the book has a different problem.

2. **Post where you already are.** Don't create a TikTok account if you've never used TikTok. Post on the platforms where you already have people. Even if it's 47 Facebook friends and a Discord server with 12 people. Those 12 people each know 12 people.

3. **One post a day, max.** Posting more doesn't help. Posting consistently does. One post. Every day. For two weeks. Then one post every 2-3 days for a month. That's the whole calendar.

4. **Reply to every comment.** Every. Single. One. Someone took time to respond to your work. That person is your marketing department. Treat them like it.

5. **Don't apologize.** Don't say "sorry for the self-promo." Don't say "I know nobody asked but." You made a thing. The thing is real. Tell people about the thing. That's not spam. That's what making things is for.

## What Actually Works (Zero Budget)

### Facebook
- Personal page posts with real stories get 10x the reach of page posts
- Local community groups (your town, your county, your church) are goldmines
- The algorithm loves comments — ask a question at the end of every post

### Twitter/X
- The thread format works for process stories
- Quote-tweet anyone who mentions your book
- Reply to people in your genre community — be a person first, an author second

### Reddit
- Contribute to communities for at least a week before posting about your book
- r/selfpublish is friendly. r/books is not (for self-promo)
- The AMA format works if you have a genuinely unusual story

### Discord
- Your own server is your home base — pin the announcement
- Other servers: ask mods before dropping links
- Be a regular first. Nobody trusts the person who shows up and immediately sells

### LinkedIn
- The "I'm not a typical author" angle works here
- Process posts outperform announcement posts
- Tag your tools (Claude, Anthropic, Raspberry Pi) — their communities amplify

## What Doesn't Work (Zero Budget)

- **Cold DMs.** Don't DM strangers about your book. Ever.
- **Posting the link with no context.** The link alone is invisible. The story is what stops the scroll.
- **Buying followers/reviews.** Amazon will catch you. Your reputation is worth more than a fake 5-star.
- **Spamming multiple groups at once.** Post in one group. See what happens. Adjust. Post in the next.
- **Comparing yourself to traditionally published authors.** Different game. Different rules. Your advantage is authenticity. Use it.

## The Minimum Viable Launch

If you can only do five things:

1. Post the announcement on your personal Facebook/Instagram
2. Post the process story on Twitter/LinkedIn
3. Drop the announcement in your Discord server
4. Post in one relevant Reddit community (after contributing first)
5. Reply to every single comment for two weeks

That's it. That's the launch. Everything else is bonus.
